# Planto 🌱

Planto is an iOS app that helps you take care of your plants. Add your plants, track which ones you've watered today, and get a daily reminder to water them.

## Main Features

- Add a plant with its name, room, light, watering days, and water amount
- Edit or delete a plant
- Swipe left on a plant to delete it
- Mark plants as watered and track your progress with a progress bar
- "All Done" screen when every plant has been watered
- Daily local notification at 9:00 AM for each plant

## Technologies Used

- Swift
- SwiftUI
- MVVM architecture (Model, ViewModel, View)
- UserNotifications (local notifications)
